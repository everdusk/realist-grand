This Privacy Policy explains how Realist Grand LTD (“we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use the Arcane Poker application (the “App”) or open these Arcane Poker pages on realistgrand.com. It applies only to Arcane Poker. It does not cover other games or pages we operate.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Realist Grand LTD, the developer of Arcane Poker.

Email: realistgrandltd.office@gmail.com

This policy: https://realistgrand.com/arcanepoker/privacy.html

2. Data we may process

  • On your device. The App stores game progress, settings, and virtual chip balances on your device. That data stays on the device. We do not receive it unless you choose to send it to us, for example in a support email.
  • Support email. If you write to us, we process the address you write from and the content of the message so we can reply and keep a record of the request.
  • Store and purchases. If you download the App or make an optional in-app purchase, the store you use (for example Google Play, the Apple App Store, or another storefront) processes your store account and payment. We receive only what that store provides to us as the developer, such as a product identifier or a confirmation that a purchase was made. We do not collect your payment card number.
  • These pages. If you open the Arcane Poker pages on realistgrand.com, the host may record technical data such as IP address, browser type, date and time of access, and the page requested, in order to deliver and secure the site.

We do not ask you to create an account with us to play Arcane Poker. We do not run real-money gambling, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Why we process data

  • to provide and support the App;
  • to respond to enquiries;
  • to honour optional purchases made through a store;
  • to operate and secure these pages;
  • to meet legal obligations.

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on legitimate interests (operating and supporting the App), performance of a contract (providing an item you purchased), and legal obligation.

4. Cookies

These pages do not set advertising cookies. The host may store strictly technical data needed to deliver them. The App does not use browser cookies. A store you use to download or pay for the App has its own privacy practices.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with the store that distributes the App, with the provider that hosts these pages, with the provider that handles our support email, and where required by law.

6. Retention

We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle your request and any related legal requirement. Hosting logs follow the host’s usual rotation. Data stored only on your device remains there until you delete the App or clear its data. Information held by a store follows that store’s retention rules.

7. International transfers

Our host, email provider, or a store may process data in other countries. Where the law requires a safeguard for that transfer, the provider’s own safeguard applies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict personal data we hold, to object to certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise rights for data we control, email realistgrandltd.office@gmail.com. Deleting the App, or clearing its storage in the device settings, removes progress stored only on the device. For data held only by a store, use that store’s privacy tools.

9. Children

Arcane Poker is for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it where we control it.

10. In-app purchases and virtual items

Optional in-app purchases may be available in the App. They are processed by the store, not by us. Virtual currency and items have no real-world cash value and cannot be won or cashed out as gambling prizes.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy. We will change the date above when we do.

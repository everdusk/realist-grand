About

The Demon Wants to Marry the Princess, or Lose Her at Poker combines poker, special abilities, and sleight of hand with managing a castle full of living cards. Win at the poker table to unlock new rooms, then prepare your deck for the next opponent back at the castle.

The King of Darkness asked for your daughter’s hand. You said no. He cursed your entire kingdom. Now only twenty cards remain of the Card World, and the princess is trapped in a distant corner of the castle. To break the curse, you must beat five demons, then deal with your would-be son-in-law himself.

Your residents are your deck. Each has their own room, story, and role in restoring the kingdom. Free rooms from the curse, discover new residents, and strengthen your cards before the final showdown.
